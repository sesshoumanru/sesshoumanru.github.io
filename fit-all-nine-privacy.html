Fit All Nine privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers Fit All Nine: Block Puzzle for iPhone and iPad, published by Bigray Studio.

Playing on your device

The game saves your current board, tutorial progress, favorite layouts, and sound and language settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. The app keeps up to ten favorite layouts. Deleting the app clears its local game data from that device.

You can play solo without a studio account or a Game Center sign-in. Version 2.0 includes no ads or third-party analytics SDKs. The studio does not operate a game server for this release.

Challenges through Game Center

When the game opens, it checks whether you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center. If you are signed in, the game can check for invitations and results. With your permission, it reads your Game Center friends list to show whom you can challenge.

When you invite a friend, Game Center handles the invitation and stores the match. The game uses player and match identifiers to deliver the board to the right friend. The players can see each other’s Game Center display names, the layout, and results such as elapsed time, moves and hints. The game sends a status update when the invited player starts the board and sends their results when they finish.

The app keeps local challenge progress and delivery records so it can resume play and retry updates. The inbox displays up to ten pending challenges per account. Apple manages the records held by Game Center separately from this device. You can change friend-list access or sign out of Game Center in your device’s Settings. For Apple’s data practices and controls, see Game Center & Privacy.

Sharing a game link

If you tap Share, the app gives Apple’s share sheet the App Store link and a board preview. You choose where to send the link. The destination app decides whether to show a preview and handles what you send under its own privacy policy.
