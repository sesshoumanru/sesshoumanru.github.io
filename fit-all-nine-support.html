Fit All Nine support

Need help with Fit All Nine: Block Puzzle on iPhone or iPad? Email sesshoumanru@hotmail.com. If you’re reporting a problem, tell us your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, what you tapped and what happened. A screenshot is helpful if you’d like to include one.

How do I fit the pieces?

Leave the seven white squares in place. Drag the nine colored pieces onto the board to fill every remaining space. Select a piece to rotate or flip it. If you get stuck, tap Hint. To try the tutorial again, open the pause menu, tap the question mark, then choose Replay tutorial.

How do I make or replay a board?

Choose Custom layout to design a board. Tap a highlighted spot for each white tile. To move a tile you’ve already placed, tap it and choose another highlighted spot. When all seven are in place, tap Start puzzle.

To keep a board you’ve finished, tap Add to favorites on the results screen. To play it again, open the pause menu and choose New round, then My favorites. The game keeps your ten most recently saved layouts.

How do I challenge a friend?

Finish a board, then tap Challenge a friend on the results screen and pick a Game Center friend. They can play later. To open an invitation, go to the pause menu and tap Received challenges.

If your friend is missing from the list, check that you are Game Center friends and both signed in on your own devices. Your friend can open Received challenges and tap Let friends find me if offered. Allow friends-list access when prompted, then tap Refresh friends on the inviting device. If an invitation is in the game but its notification was quiet, check Focus and notification settings on the receiving device.
